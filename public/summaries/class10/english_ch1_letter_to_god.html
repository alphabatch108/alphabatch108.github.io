Class 10 English • Chapter 1

A Letter to God by G.L. Fuentes — Chapter Summary

Complete Chapter Overview, Theme & Key Character Analysis.

"God, if you don't help me, my family and I will go hungry this year. I need a hundred pesos in order to sow my field again..."

1. The Protagonist & Setting

Lencho was a hardworking farmer living in a solitary house on the crest of a low hill. His cornfields promised a rich harvest, but he anxiously awaited rain for a good crop yield.

2. The Destruction by Hailstorm

A heavy rain began, which initially filled Lencho with joy. However, the rain quickly turned into a devastating hailstorm. Large hailstones covered the entire valley like salt, destroying the corn fields and leaves completely.

3. Unshakable Faith in God

Despite losing everything, Lencho possessed immense faith in God. He wrote a letter directly addressed to God requesting 100 pesos to resow his fields and sustain his family until the next harvest.

4. The Postmaster's Act of Kindness

The postman and postmaster were deeply moved by Lencho's innocent faith. The postmaster collected money from employees and friends, contributing part of his own salary to raise 70 pesos.

5. The Irony of the Ending

When Lencho received 70 pesos instead of 100, he became angry thinking the post office employees had stolen the remaining 30 pesos. He wrote a second letter asking God to send the rest of the money, but not through the mail because he called the post office employees a "bunch of crooks."
