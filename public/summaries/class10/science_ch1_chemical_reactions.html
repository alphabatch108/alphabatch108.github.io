Chemical Reactions & Equations — Complete Chapter Summary

Official Revision Notes & HTML Summary by Alpha Arts Editorial Team.

Core Concept: A chemical reaction takes place when one or more reactants undergo transformation to form new products with distinct chemical properties.

1. Signs of a Chemical Reaction

  • Change in state (e.g., liquid to gas)
  • Change in color
  • Evolution of a gas (e.g., H₂ gas in zinc + acid reaction)
  • Change in temperature (exothermic or endothermic)

2. Balanced Chemical Equations

Law of Conservation of Mass states that mass can neither be created nor destroyed in a chemical reaction. Therefore, the total number of atoms of each element remains equal on both sides of a chemical equation.

Example: Fe + H₂O → Fe₃O₄ + H₂ when balanced becomes 3Fe + 4H₂O → Fe₃O₄ + 4H₂.

3. Types of Chemical Reactions

  • Combination Reaction: Two or more reactants combine to form a single product. (e.g., CaO + H₂O → Ca(OH)₂)
  • Decomposition Reaction: A single reactant breaks down into simpler products using heat, light, or electricity.
  • Displacement Reaction: A more reactive element displaces a less reactive element from its solution. (e.g., Fe + CuSO₄ → FeSO₄ + Cu)
  • Double Displacement Reaction: Exchange of ions between reactants takes place forming a precipitate.
  • Oxidation and Reduction (Redox): Oxidation is gain of oxygen or loss of hydrogen. Reduction is loss of oxygen or gain of hydrogen.

4. Corrosion & Rancidity

Corrosion: Degradation of metals due to air, moisture, or chemicals (e.g., rusting of iron). Rancidity: Oxidation of fats and oils in food changing taste and odor.
