Class 12 English Core • Flamingo Lesson 1

The Last Lesson by Alphonse Daudet — Detailed HTML Summary

Comprehensive Revision Notes, Theme of Linguistic Chauvinism & Character Notes.

Theme: The importance of mother tongue, the pain of losing freedom, linguistic chauvinism, and procrastination in learning.

1. Introduction & Background

Set during the Franco-Prussian War (1870-1871), France was defeated by Prussia led by Bismarck. The French districts of Alsace and Lorraine passed into Prussian hands. An order came from Berlin that only German would be taught in schools of Alsace and Lorraine.

2. Franz's Fear & Observation

Franz, a schoolboy, started late for school fearing M. Hamel's scolding on participles. On his way, he saw a crowd gathered around the town hall bulletin board. Upon arriving at school, instead of usual commotion, everything was strangely quiet like a Sunday morning.

3. The Unusual Classroom Atmosphere

M. Hamel was dressed in his finest green coat and frilled shirt, reserved for inspection days. Elderly village people (Old Hauser, former mayor, former postmaster) sat quietly on back benches showing respect for 40 years of M. Hamel's faithful service.

4. M. Hamel's Announcement

M. Hamel announced softly that this was their last French lesson. The news hit Franz like a thunderclap. He instantly regretted taking French for granted, playing instead of studying his books.

5. Significance of French Language

M. Hamel praised French as the most beautiful, clearest, and most logical language in the world. He urged the people to guard it, stating: "When a people are enslaved, as long as they hold fast to their language, it is as if they had the key to their prison."

6. The Emotional Conclusion

As the church clock struck twelve and Prussian trumpets sounded, M. Hamel stood up, choked with emotion. Unable to speak further, he turned to the blackboard, took a piece of chalk, and wrote in large letters: "Vive La France!" (Long Live France!).
